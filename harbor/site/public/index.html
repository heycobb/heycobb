Your NAS on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Arkenal opens the files on your Synology, or any NAS that shares over SMB or WebDAV. It connects to the NAS directly: there's no account to make and no cloud service in between.

Open what's on it

Photos, PDFs, documents and archives open where they are. Video and music stream straight from the NAS, so a long film starts playing right away, and it can keep playing in Picture in Picture while you use other apps.

Upload from your camera roll or drag files in on a Mac or iPad. Rename, move, compress, search every shared folder at once, or send a share link from a Synology.

Back up your photos

Photo Backup copies your photos and videos to a folder on the NAS as originals: HEIC stays HEIC, sorted into year and month folders. It remembers what's already there, so nothing is uploaded twice, even when you back up from an iPhone and a Mac that share iCloud Photos.

Run it with Back Up Now, or let it run on its own in the background (Pro). The Photos tab then shows everything on the NAS by month, like the Photos app.

In Files and Finder

Your shared folders appear in the Files app on iPhone and iPad, and in the Finder sidebar on a Mac. Other apps can open and save files on the NAS directly, and folders you choose stay downloaded for when you're offline.

Encrypted folders

Files in an encrypted folder are encrypted on your device before they reach the NAS, with AES-256 from Apple's own CryptoKit. The NAS stores scrambled names and contents; your password, Face ID or a recovery key unlocks them in Arkenal. (Pro)

Away from home

Connect over Tailscale or another VPN, Synology's QuickConnect, or your own domain. Arkenal tries your home address first and switches when you leave. It checks the NAS's certificate and stops if it ever changes unexpectedly.

Works with

Synology DSM 7
Everything, including QuickConnect, 2-factor sign-in, share links and the Recycle Bin.
Other NAS, over SMB
Most NAS share files this way, including QNAP, TrueNAS, Unraid and UGREEN, as do Macs and Windows PCs. SMB isn't encrypted on most NAS, so use it at home or over a VPN.
WebDAV and Nextcloud
Type your Nextcloud's address and Arkenal finds the rest.

Privacy

Arkenal collects nothing. There's no account, no analytics and no advertising, and we don't run a server your files could pass through: they go only between your devices and your NAS. The privacy policy has the details.

Questions

Can I try it without a NAS?

Yes. On first launch, choose Try the Demo Library to look around with sample files.

Is the Mac version included?

Yes. Arkenal and Arkenal Pro are one purchase for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

What does my NAS need?

A Synology needs DSM 7, with File Station turned on (it is by default). Other NAS need SMB or WebDAV turned on. Arkenal adds nothing to the NAS.

Do I need QuickConnect or a Synology account?

No. At home Arkenal connects over your network, and away from home it can use a VPN like Tailscale, your own domain, or QuickConnect if you already use it.

Something isn't working.
